NextPeriod

Quick answers

Is NextPeriod free?

Yes. NextPeriod is free to use. Donations are optional and help support continued development.

Can I use English or Thai?

NextPeriod supports both English and Thai. You can choose the language that works best for you.

What if class reminders do not arrive?

Check that notifications are allowed for NextPeriod in your device settings and that reminders are enabled in the app. If the issue continues, email us with your device type and a short description.

What if my timetable is not syncing?

Check your internet connection and reopen NextPeriod on the affected devices. If your timetable still does not update, email us with the devices involved and what you see on each one.
